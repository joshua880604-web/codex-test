## Editorial scope

Each entry answers the definition directly, states a formula and its units, provides original numerical steps, highlights a specific misconception, explains the linked tool's actual scope, and names the sources consulted. Source text was accessed through web tools on 2026-10-22. No source text was copied as an article. Publication/check dates are fixed when the source/arithmetic check actually occurs, not generated afresh on every build.

- Compound interest: fixed-rate illustration, comparison with simple interest, frequency and negative-return limitations.
- CAGR: endpoints/annual compounding, no external cash flows, not arithmetic averaging or a volatility measure.
- Real return: exact Fisher relationship, same-period rates, distinction between inflation, fees and tax.
- Expense ratio: annual operating-cost percentage, simplified deduction timing and net-return double-counting caveat.
- DCA: three price-varying purchases, share-weighted cost and explicit loss; the regular-contribution Investment Calculator is not presented as a share-price backtest.
- 401(k): U.S. scope, traditional/Roth basics, hypothetical employer match, vesting and changing annual limits. Link to current IRS limits without duplicating an undated limit in the definition.
- Amortization: principal/interest split, positive-rate fixed-payment illustration, rounding and escrow exclusions.
- Markup vs. margin: correct denominators, reverse target-price example, gross versus net profit and consistent tax basis.

Seven examples have calculator presets with declared expected results; the DCA share-count example is verified independently and deliberately has no misleading replay link. Presets contain only published synthetic example inputs. Existing user-input sharing behavior is unchanged.
