### 401(k) Projection (projection)

Local inputs: age, retire, life, salary, balance, contribution, match, match_limit, salary_growth, return, inflation.

Local formula: For each year: employee = min(annual salary × contribution%, age-based annual limit); employer = min(employee, annual salary × match limit%) × match%; ending balance = opening balance × (1 + annual return) + employee + employer. Starting balance plus employee contributions = starting 401(k) balance + sum of annual employee amounts.

Local default outputs: Projected 401(k) balance; Employee contributions; Starting 401(k) balance; Starting balance plus employee contributions; Employer contributions; Inflation-adjusted balance; Initial monthly retirement withdrawal; Initial withdrawal in today’s dollars; Total retirement withdrawals.

### Early Withdrawal Cost (early)

Local inputs: withdrawal, federal, state, local, employed, separated55, disabled, exception.

Local formula: Net withdrawal = gross − marginal income tax − applicable 10% additional tax.

Local default outputs: Net amount received; Estimated income tax; Additional early withdrawal tax.

### Maximize Employer Match (match)

Local inputs: age, salary, match1, limit1, match2, limit2.

Local formula: Employer match = first band × first match rate + additional band × second match rate.

Local default outputs: Minimum contribution rate for full match; Maximum steady contribution rate; Age-based annual employee limit (2026); Annual contribution to maximize match; Contribution per month; Employer annual match; Total annual additions.
